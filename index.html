An app for working it.

Set up your congregation once from any browser, control exactly what each publisher can access,
and let them work their territory offline with no account of their own.

Why choose Service Sheep?

  • Browser-based, no installation

    Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Nothing to download or update.

  • You control who has access

    Grant each publisher exactly the features they need — nothing more, nothing hidden.

  • Set up once per congregation

    Your congregation gets a single console, seeded to the Service Overseer from day one.

  • Your publishers’ app works offline

    Once a map is imported, publishers can work it with no internet connection at all.

Everything you need to manage your congregation’s territory

  • Territory & Map Management

    Create territories, draw or import map boundaries, and assign maps to publishers or field service groups — with overdue tracking built in.

  • Publisher Management

    Add every publisher once, then grant each one exactly the permissions they need. No fixed roles — you decide, person by person.

  • Device Enrollment

    Generate a QR code and passphrase for each publisher, and enroll their device together, in person.

  • Reports & Coverage

    Review incoming visit reports, see which maps are overdue, and download reports for your congregation’s records.
